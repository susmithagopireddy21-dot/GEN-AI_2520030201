# Week 4 – Tokenizer Lab

## Aim

To compare three popular subword tokenization methods — BPE, WordPiece and SentencePiece — using English, Telugu and Hindi sentences.

## Introduction

Tokenization is the process of breaking text into smaller units called tokens. These tokens are converted into numerical IDs so that language models can process text.

In this practical, I used three pretrained tokenizers and compared how they split sentences in different languages.

## Tokenizers Used

1. **BPE – GPT-2**
   - Model: `gpt2`
   - Uses Byte Pair Encoding.
   - Frequently occurring character pairs are merged to create subword units.

2. **WordPiece – Multilingual BERT**
   - Model: `bert-base-multilingual-cased`
   - Splits words into smaller subword pieces when the complete word is not available in its vocabulary.

3. **SentencePiece – XLM-R**
   - Model: `xlm-roberta-base`
   - Uses SentencePiece tokenization with a BPE model.
   - Supports multiple languages.

## Languages Used

The following sentences are used for comparison:

- **English:** Artificial intelligence is changing the world.
- **Telugu:** కృత్రిమ మేధస్సు ప్రపంచాన్ని మారుస్తోంది.
- **Hindi:** कृत्रिम बुद्धिमत्ता दुनिया को बदल रही है।
